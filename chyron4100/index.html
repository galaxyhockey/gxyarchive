Type to write on the preview. Arrows move, Shift+←/→ selects, Enter starts a new row, Tab jumps rows, Ctrl+arrows position the row (add Shift for bigger steps). Function keys ask for a number on the status line: type it and press Enter, or Esc to cancel.

Messages are numbered 0001 to 9999 and saved on disk A in this browser. Sample messages 1001 to 1006 are fictional.

Fonts 1 to 4 are ITC Korinna, the classic 4100-era broadcast face. It is a commercial font, so it is not included. Press Load Fonts and pick your Korinna regular and bold files (.otf or .ttf); they stay in this browser. Until then a marked substitute is shown.
